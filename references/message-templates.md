# Investor Outreach Message Templates

Use this library when drafting investor LinkedIn and email outreach. Do not treat one template as canonical by default. Generate candidate variants, show them to the user for approval, then select:

- 1 universal base template
- 2 niche templates for specific investor/persona groups
- backup variants kept for later regeneration

Investor-facing language is English unless the user asks otherwise.

## Rules

- Keep emails human: `Hi FirstName,` then short paragraphs and `Best, [FounderName]`.
- Do not use source-list/database phrasing in investor-facing copy.
- Keep source-list rationale in `Internal Notes`.
- Personalize with real evidence only: thesis, portfolio, role, sector, geography, or relevant public signal.
- Never invent warm intros, portfolio links, customer names, revenue, or traction.
- Preserve all viable templates in `message_template_candidates.json`.
- Save the user-approved selected set in `approved_message_templates.json`.

## LinkedIn Connection Request Templates

### 1. First-Time Founder Seeking Angel Investors

```text
Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name], founder of [Startup]. We're solving [problem] in [industry] with our unique approach. I've been following your investments in [relevant area] and believe our vision aligns with your thesis. Would love to connect and share our journey.
```

### 2. Founder With Early Traction Seeking Seed Funding

```text
Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] from [Startup]. We've achieved [X]% growth in [time period] and are now raising our seed round. Your experience with [relevant portfolio company] makes me think you'd see our potential. Would you be open to connecting?
```

### 3. Founder in a Competitive Space With a Unique Differentiator

```text
Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name], founder of [Startup]. In the crowded [industry] space, we've developed a [unique differentiator] that sets us apart. Given your investments in [relevant sector], I'd value your perspective on our approach. Mind connecting?
```

### 4. Technical Founder Seeking Technical Investors

```text
Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name], a [technical background] with [X] years in [industry]. My co-founder and I built [Startup] to solve [problem] with our [technical innovation]. Your technical background and investments in deep tech caught my attention. Let's connect?
```

### 5. Founder With Industry Experience Seeking Strategic Investors

```text
Hi [Name], After [X] years in [industry] at [previous company], I founded [Startup] to solve [inefficiency]. Your expertise with [relevant portfolio company] would be invaluable as we scale. Would you be open to connecting and sharing insights?
```

### 6. Founder Pivoting Their Business Model

```text
Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] from [Startup]. We've pivoted from [original model] to [new model] after discovering [new opportunity]. Your investment thesis around [relevant theme] aligns perfectly with our evolved direction. Mind connecting?
```

### 7. Founder With Impressive Early Metrics/Growth

```text
Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] from [Startup]. We've achieved [X] revenue in [Y] months with [Z]% margins, all without marketing spend. Your experience scaling [relevant portfolio company] would be invaluable. Would you connect to discuss our growth?
```

### 8. Founder in a Niche Market Seeking Specialized Investors

```text
Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] from [Startup]. While most focus on [broader market], we're targeting the underserved $[X] [niche market]. I noticed your investment in [relevant niche company] and thought you'd appreciate our focused approach. Can we set up a quick chat?
```

### 9. Founder of a Social Impact Startup

```text
Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] from [Startup]. We've impacted [number] lives through our [solution], addressing [social problem]. Your work with [relevant impact investment] aligns with our mission. Would you connect to discuss creating impact with returns?
```

### 10. Founder Following Up After an Initial Meeting

```text
Hi [Name], Great meeting you at [event]! I enjoyed our conversation about [topic]. Since then, [Startup] has [new development]. I'd love to continue our discussion and explore how you might be involved. Mind connecting?
```

## LinkedIn First Messages After Connect

### 1. Mission-Aligned Introduction

```text
Hi [Name], thanks for connecting!
At [Startup], we're on a mission to [mission statement]. Given your work with [relevant portfolio company], I'd love to share how we're [specific approach] to solve [problem].
When might you have 15 minutes for a quick chat?
```

### 2. Product-Focused Opener

```text
Great to connect, [Name]!
Our product [product name] helps [target audience] achieve [key outcome]. Since you invest in [industry], I thought you'd appreciate our unique [differentiator].
Would you be open to a brief demo next week?
```

### 3. Shared Interest Angle

```text
Hi [Name], thanks for connecting!
I noticed we both share an interest in [shared interest/industry trend]. At [Startup], we're applying this to solve [problem] through [solution]. Would love to hear your perspective.
Free for a quick call?
```

### 4. Traction Highlight

```text
Thanks for connecting, [Name]!
Since launching [product], we've [key traction metric] by [percentage]. Given your expertise in [relevant area], I'd value your insights on our growth strategy.
Any time for a 15-min chat next week?
```

### 5. Problem-Solution Frame

```text
Hi [Name], great to connect!
We founded [Startup] to solve [pain point] that [affected group] faces daily. Our solution [briefly describe] has already [validation].
Would you be open to discussing how we're addressing this gap?
```

### 6. Industry Insight Hook

```text
Thanks for connecting, [Name]!
With all the changes in [industry], we're seeing [specific trend] creating opportunities. At [Startup], we're positioned to [capitalize on trend] through [approach].
Curious to hear your thoughts?
```

### 7. Personalized Compliment

```text
Hi [Name], thanks for connecting!
I really admired your work on [specific achievement/project]. At [Startup], we're taking a similar approach to [related problem] by [method].
Would love to compare notes sometime?
```

### 8. Customer Success Story

```text
Great to connect, [Name]!
We just helped [customer type] achieve [specific result] using [product]. Given your investments in [relevant space], I thought you'd find this case study interesting.
Open to sharing details?
```

### 9. Vision Statement

```text
Hi [Name], thanks for connecting!
We envision a future where [vision statement]. At [Startup], we're building [product] to make this happen. Given your forward-thinking investments, I'd love to discuss our roadmap.
Any time next week?
```

### 10. Collaborative Approach

```text
Thanks for connecting, [Name]!
We're always looking for smart partners to help solve [problem]. At [Startup], we've developed [solution] that [key benefit].
Would you be open to exploring synergies over coffee or a call?
```

## Email Base Templates

Use these as starting points, then adapt to the company profile and investor fit.

### Universal Short Deck Ask

```text
Hi [FirstName],

I'm reaching out because [Fund] looks relevant for [Startup] given [specific but natural reason].

[Startup] helps growth teams turn competitor ads, organic trends, and market signals into data-driven creative decisions before launch. We launched in March, have 10 paying customers, and are raising a $500k+ pre-seed round.

Could I send over a short deck?

Best,
[FounderName]
```

### Scope Check for Uncertain-Fit Funds

```text
Hi [FirstName],

[Fund] came up in our search for software and marketing technology investors, and I wanted to check if [Startup] is within your current scope.

We help growth teams use AI agents to analyze competitor ads and organic trends before deciding what creative concepts to test next. We launched in March, have 10 paying customers, and are raising a $500k+ pre-seed round.

If this is within scope, could I send over a short deck?

Best,
[FounderName]
```

### Traction-First Variant

```text
Hi [FirstName],

[Startup] has [traction proof] and is using [specific product approach] to solve [investor-relevant problem].

We're raising a $500k+ pre-seed round to scale the product and outbound motion. [Fund] looks relevant given [specific investor fit reason].

Could I send over a short deck?

Best,
[FounderName]
```
